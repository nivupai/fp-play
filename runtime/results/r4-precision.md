# R4: per-layer precision policies

Generated by `fp-play-runtime experiment --out results`. Every number below comes from that run.

## Setup

- Model: the R3 decoder dimensions (hidden 256, 2 layers, 16 query and 8 KV heads, FFN 704, vocabulary 2048, 2000128 parameters) with FP32 master weights: R3's random draws rounded once to binary32.
- Arithmetic: every matrix product is a fused chain in its site's accumulator format (exact product, one rounding per term, from +0, input index in order) on weights and input activations rounded to the site's storage format. The attention core (scores and the weighted sum of V) is a fused FP32 chain over the KV cache's stored values. RMSNorm, RoPE, softmax, SiLU and residual adds are binary32; the residual stream is binary32.
- Levels: `fp32` (FP32 storage and accumulation), `bf16` (BF16 storage, FP32 accumulation), `bf16-acc` (BF16 storage and accumulation), `fp16`, `fp16-acc`. Sites: each layer's attention projections (Q, K, V, O), each layer's MLP (gate, up, down), the KV cache (storage only), and the tied embedding and head.
- Workload: 8 prompts of 16 tokens. The reference (all `fp32`) decodes 32 tokens greedily; every policy is then run teacher-forced on those tokens, so all see the same contexts, and compared at every continuation token (256 in all). Separately each policy decodes greedily on its own.
- Metrics, in binary64 from the binary32 logits: max and mean RMS logit difference from the reference; mean KL(p_ref ‖ p_policy); top-1 agreement; the perplexity of the reference's continuation (the reference's own is 82.5093). With random weights the perplexity is a self-consistency measure, not a language-modeling result.

## What this does and does not show

- It shows how much each site's storage format and accumulation width moves the logits of one small decoder, with every rounding of every matrix product executed exactly as specified.
- The weights are random and the model has 2 layers; a trained or deeper model can rank its sites differently. The perplexity is that of the reference's own greedy continuation, not of text.
- Only matrix products, their inputs and the KV cache change format; element-wise work, the residual stream and the attention core stay binary32. FP8 and INT8 with scales are not modeled.
- Every sum runs in one sequential order. A narrow accumulator's error depends on that order; blocked or tree-shaped kernels were not modeled.
