## What this does and does not show

- The spread is across input seeds for one synthetic distribution (Irwin–Hall normal, weights scaled by 1/√hidden). Other distributions, real activations or outliers may behave differently.
- Runs are reduced to 1024 elements to keep the sweep a normal test; one element is 0.10% of a run, so small percentages are a few elements.
- The shard tree's invariance is across P for a fixed S, and its local kernel computes S/P partials per device, a different summation order from the other schedules' per-device kernel.
- Top-1 changes are over only a few tokens and 256 outputs, not a vocabulary; whether bit differences change greedy decoding is an R3 experiment. No hardware or interconnect measurement is claimed.
